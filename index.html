My Dearest Darling,

I want to take a moment to express my deepest gratitude and love for you. This year has been a challenging year for us handling two kids, and yet you have handled everything with such grace, strength, and love. Thank you for giving birth to our beautiful second daughter. She is so cute, healthy and strong. It is such a God-given blessing that we manage to have two kids just as how we envisioned before our marriage.

I also want to thank you for covering so many of the parenting duties when my left arm was injured. You helped me to bath and take care of our kids even when things felt overwhelming. Your love for our girls shines through in everything you do, and I am beyond grateful for you.

Thank you for making such a wise decision in hiring our 2nd helper. She has been such a help, allowing us both to focus on work and on each other without being overwhelmed by daily tasks. Your tremendous effort for helper hunt has paid off and your thoughtfulness has ensured that our family is well supported.

Through it all, I see God blessing our lives. I'm so thankful for His blessings over us, and for His love and grace that carry us through this season of life.

Chee Ling, I want to reaffirm my commitment that I will always love you and cherish you. We've already been together for 7 years (including dating years) and I look forward to growing old with you by my side. No matter what comes our way, we will face it together, with love and faith.
