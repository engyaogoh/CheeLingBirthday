给你的信 · THE LETTER

今年，我想对你说的

生日快乐。除了这首歌，还有一封每年都会写的信。

Happy birthday to my beloved wife!!!

I want to take this opportunity to thank you for everything you have done for our family and for loving me all these years. This year has not been easy for us as we continue to handle Esther and Lydia, but you have continued to care for them with so much patience, strength, and love.

This is our 7 years together, and I can truly say that I am deeper in love with you and cherish you more as each year passes. From dating, getting married, buying our home, going through pregnancy, and becoming parents to Esther and Lydia, we have already gone through many different seasons of life together. You are still the lovely, strong, thoughtful, and wonderful lady that I fell in love with. In fact, I love you even more now because I have seen more of who you are and how much love you give to our family.

I thank God for all the blessings in our life. I am thankful for our two beautiful daughters, our financial provision, our jobs, our home, our two helpers, and all the support that God has given us. Most importantly, I thank God for giving me you. You are the greatest blessing in my life, and I do not want to take you for granted. When I look at our family, I know that we have received much more than what we could have planned or imagined before our marriage.

Bringing up children is a lifetime journey. There will be happy moments, tiring moments, difficult moments, and moments where we do not know what to do. But I am happy that I get to go through this journey with you. We are not perfect parents, but we can continue to learn, pray, and support each other. I also promise you that I will be more involved in disciplining our daughters. I know that this should not be something that you need to handle alone. I will try to be more consistent, patient, and actively involved so that we can guide Esther and Lydia together.

Darling, thank you for being my wife, my partner, and the mother of our children. Even though life is busy and we are often tired, I still want us to continue making time for each other, exercising together, praying together, and enjoying our life as husband and wife. Let us continue to grow old together and become more in love with each other as the years go by.

I love you very much, darling. Happy birthday once again. May God continue to bless you with good health, joy, strength, and peace. You are very precious to me, and I will continue to love, cherish, and take care of you for the rest of my life.

From: Eng Yao
